### Position-sizing calculations

For a long trade:

Risk per share = Entry − Stop-loss.

Reward per share = Target − Entry.

For a short trade:

Risk per share = Stop-loss − Entry.

Reward per share = Entry − Target.

Quantity by risk = FLOOR(Risk budget / Risk per share).

Quantity by capital = FLOOR(Available capital / Entry price).

Final quantity = MIN(Quantity by risk, Quantity by capital), provided the values are valid and the instrument's execution requirements are satisfied.

Capital deployed = Final quantity × Entry price.

Cash remaining = Available capital − Capital deployed.

Planned gross loss = Final quantity × Risk per share.

Planned gross target profit = Final quantity × Reward per share.

Estimated net target profit = Planned gross target profit − estimated costs.

For a conservative estimated net reward-to-risk ratio, if the costs input represents the total estimated round-trip cost:

Estimated net risk = Planned gross loss + estimated costs.

Estimated net reward-to-risk = Estimated net target profit / Estimated net risk.

Clearly label these calculations as estimates.

If costs cannot be reliably estimated, mark them as unverified. Do not imply that gross reward-to-risk is the same as net reward-to-risk.

### Validation rules

For a long trade, require:

Stop-loss < Entry < Target.

For a short trade, require:

Target < Entry < Stop-loss.

Reject zero or negative prices, invalid quantities, negative costs, and invalid capital or risk values.

If the quantity is zero, show "Do not trade — position size is zero."

Warn if the estimated net reward-to-risk is below 2:1.

Warn if the planned risk exceeds the configured risk budget.

Never automatically modify a logical stop-loss to increase the position size.

Display the calculations clearly and update them when the user changes inputs.

Include buttons:
- Calculate Position
- Copy Trade Plan
- Save Plan to Journal
- Clear Form

Copy Trade Plan must copy a readable, plain-text summary to the clipboard.

## 9. ACTUAL P&L CALCULATIONS

Use actual entry, actual exit, actual quantity, and actual total costs.

For a long trade:

Gross P&L = (Actual exit − Actual entry) × Actual quantity.

For a short trade:

Gross P&L = (Actual entry − Actual exit) × Actual quantity.

Net P&L = Gross P&L − Actual total costs.

Use actual fills, not planned prices, for realized performance.

An open trade must not be counted as a closed, realized trade in win rate or realized net P&L.

Validate that a trade cannot be marked Closed without actual entry, exit, and quantity.

Handle breakeven trades separately from wins and losses.

Use consistent currency rounding for display while preserving sufficient precision for calculations.

Definitions:
- Win rate = winning closed trades / all closed trades × 100, with breakeven trades included in the denominator.

- Maximum drawdown = largest peak-to-subsequent-trough decline in cumulative realized net P&L, calculated chronologically.
